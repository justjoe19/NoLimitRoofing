About Us

Built On Craftsmanship, Not Shortcuts

No Limit Roofing is a locally owned and operated, fully licensed, bonded and insured roofing contractor serving the Michiana region since 2010.

Our Story

25+ Years Of Experience, One Name You Can Trust

Founded in 2010, No Limit Roofing was built on a simple idea: give homeowners and business owners a roofing contractor who shows up, does the job right, and stands behind the work. Owner Jonathan Smith brings more than 25 years of roofing experience to every project and personally supervises each crew — from the first inspection to the final walkthrough.

Today, No Limit Roofing operates regional offices in Mishawaka and Plymouth, Indiana, with an Ohio location opening soon, specializing in residential, commercial and industrial roofing restoration across the greater South Bend–Elkhart region.
