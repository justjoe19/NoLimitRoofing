FAQ

Common Roofing Questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every inspection and written estimate is free with no obligation, whether it's a small repair or a full commercial roof replacement.

How fast can you respond to an emergency roof repair?

We prioritize emergency calls for active leaks and storm damage and aim to get a crew out quickly to stop further damage. Call (574) 360-0525 for the fastest response.

What roofing materials do you install?

We install asphalt and composite shingles, metal roofing, wood shakes, tile, and flat/low-slope systems including EPDM, TPO, PVC, KEE and self-adhered modified bitumen.

Are you licensed, bonded and insured?

Yes — No Limit Roofing is fully licensed, bonded and insured, and is a GAF factory-certified contractor.

Do you work on commercial and industrial buildings?

Yes. We handle residential, commercial and industrial roofing, including flat roof systems for commercial properties.
