## Where Commercial Roofing Matters Most

Elkhart is the biggest city in Elkhart County, built around the meeting of the Elkhart and St. Joseph rivers. It is known nationally as the RV capital of the world, and manufacturing and industrial buildings are a big part of the local landscape.

For roofing, that means a large share of the roofs here are big, flat or low-slope commercial roofs rather than steep shingle roofs. These roofs age differently, leak differently, and call for different materials, and getting the choice right can mean many years of difference in how long the roof holds up.

## Commercial Roof Options We Work With

- **[TPO](/commercial/tpo.html).** A single-ply membrane with heat-welded seams and a reflective surface, common on industrial and retail buildings.
- **[EPDM](/commercial/epdm.html).** A rubber membrane with a long track record, often chosen for its durability and value. Our guide to [TPO versus EPDM](/learning-center/tpo-vs-epdm-for-commercial-buildings.html) explains how owners decide.
- **[Roof coatings](/commercial/roof-coatings.html).** For a roof that is still basically sound, a coating can sometimes extend its life without a full tear-off. See when a [coating makes more sense than replacement](/learning-center/when-does-a-commercial-roof-need-coating-vs-replacement.html).
- **[Replacement](/commercial/replacement.html), [repair](/commercial/repair.html) and [maintenance](/commercial/maintenance.html).** From a single leaking seam to a full re-roof.

Regular maintenance is the least glamorous and often the most cost-effective option. A yearly look at seams, drains and flashing catches small issues before they leak into inventory or equipment.

## Storms And Leaks

The same summer storm season that hits the rest of the region applies here. Wind can lift a membrane edge or flashing on a large flat roof, and hail can damage both membranes and shingles. We offer [wind damage](/storm-damage/wind-damage.html) and [hail damage](/storm-damage/hail-damage.html) inspections with photo documentation, and [emergency repair](/roofing/emergency-roof-repair.html) when something can't wait. If you are filing an insurance claim, our written estimate can support the conversation, though your insurer makes the final call.
