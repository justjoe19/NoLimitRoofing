## A Younger Kind Of Roofing Market

Granger sits just east of Mishawaka and is best known as a suburban community rather than a city. Much of the area was built up in newer subdivisions, so the typical Granger roof is often younger than the ones we see in the older parts of the county.

That changes the conversation. Instead of "is this roof finished?", the questions here tend to be "did that storm hurt it?", "is this leak coming from flashing?", and "what should we put on when the time comes?"

## Common Roofing Questions In Granger

- **Storm damage on a roof that looks fine.** Hail can bruise shingles and wind can break the seal on them without shingles blowing off. Our guides on [what hail damage looks like](/learning-center/what-does-hail-damage-look-like.html) and [wind damage without blown-off shingles](/learning-center/can-wind-damage-shingles-without-blowing-off.html) show what inspectors look for.
- **Leaks at details.** On homes with several rooflines, valleys, dormers, chimneys and skylights, the trouble usually starts at the joins and penetrations, not the flat shingle field. A targeted [roof repair](/roofing/roof-repair.html) often solves it.
- **Ventilation and attic conditions.** Poor airflow shortens shingle life and feeds ice dams. We check it during [roof inspections](/roofing/roof-inspections.html).

## If Your Roof Was Hit By A Storm

After a hail or wind event, don't wait for a leak to show up. We can inspect the roof and document damage with photos, then give you a written estimate. You can share it with your insurance company, which makes the claim conversation easier. We cover the process on our [hail damage](/storm-damage/hail-damage.html) and [wind damage](/storm-damage/wind-damage.html) pages. The claim decision is always your insurer's, and we're not a substitute for their adjuster.
