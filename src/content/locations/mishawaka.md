No Limit Roofing has been based in Mishawaka since 2010. Our office on Clover Road is the place our estimates, scheduling and crews work out of, so this city is not a far-off market for us. ## What Roofs Deal With Here

Mishawaka is in the snowbelt influence of Lake Michigan, and the St. Joseph River runs through town. A few things follow from that:

- **Freeze-thaw cycling.** Snow melts on a warm afternoon, then refreezes at night at the eaves. Over the years that can lift shingles, open up flashing, and cause ice dams. Our guide to [ice and water shield](/learning-center/what-does-ice-and-water-shield-do.html) explains one of the best defenses.
- **Summer storms.** Hail and straight-line winds are behind many of the storm damage calls across the county. Damage is not always visible from the ground, so a proper inspection matters.
- **Aging roofs.** Roofs tend to wear out gradually, with granule loss, curling shingles and failing flashing. Catching this early usually keeps the fix a repair instead of a replacement.

## Homes And Buildings We Roof

The city has a mix of older homes closer to the river and downtown, and newer subdivisions on the edges. Older houses sometimes have decking that needs attention once the old shingles come off, and we cover [what happens if bad decking is found](/learning-center/what-happens-if-bad-decking-is-found.html) so there are no surprises. Newer homes often have more complex rooflines, and those benefit from careful flashing work.

Mishawaka also has plenty of shops, offices and small commercial buildings, many with flat or low-slope roofs. For those we install and repair [TPO](/commercial/tpo.html) and [EPDM](/commercial/epdm.html) membranes, and we offer scheduled [commercial roof maintenance](/commercial/maintenance.html) to catch small problems before they become leaks.
