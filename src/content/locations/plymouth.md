## Rooted In Marshall County

Plymouth is the county seat of Marshall County, on the Yellow River in northern Indiana. It's best known locally for events like the Blueberry Festival, and it is surrounded by farmland. It is also one of our two regional offices. No Limit Roofing has been working from Plymouth since 2010, so this is not a place we drive out to occasionally.

## What Open Country Means For A Roof

Plymouth sits at the southern edge of our core service area, and it shares the region's freeze-thaw winters and summer storm season. What's different is exposure. With fewer buildings and trees to break the wind, rural and edge-of-town roofs tend to take wind more directly.

Wind damage often doesn't look dramatic. Shingles can crease, lift or lose their seal without blowing off, and water then finds its way in later. Our guide on [wind damage that doesn't blow shingles off](/learning-center/can-wind-damage-shingles-without-blowing-off.html) explains what to look for, and our [wind damage](/storm-damage/wind-damage.html) and [hail damage](/storm-damage/hail-damage.html) pages describe how we inspect.

## Roofs We See Around Plymouth

- **Town homes.** Established houses in and around Plymouth often need the same things: fresh flashing, replaced shingles, and ventilation fixes. See [roof repair](/roofing/roof-repair.html).
- **Rural homes.** Larger lots and open land mean more wind, and sometimes longer runs of roofing on larger homes.
- **Commercial buildings.** Shops, offices and light industrial buildings usually have low-slope roofs. We offer [commercial repair](/commercial/repair.html), [replacement](/commercial/replacement.html) and [maintenance](/commercial/maintenance.html).

When a roof is near the end of its life, our [roof replacement](/roofing/roof-replacement.html) page explains what to expect, and [asphalt shingle roofing](/roofing/asphalt-shingle-roofing.html) covers the most common option.

After a storm, out-of-town contractors often show up in a region and are hard to find a year later. Working from a local office is one way we stay accountable for our work.
