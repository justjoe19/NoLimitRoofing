Replacing a roof is one of the largest investments a homeowner makes in a property, and it's not a job for shortcuts. No Limit Roofing has handled full residential roof replacements across South Bend, Mishawaka, Plymouth and the greater Michiana region since 2010, and every replacement follows the same process regardless of the size of the home: a full tear-off to the decking, an honest look at what's underneath, and a factory-certified installation from a crew Jonathan Smith personally supervises.

A full replacement means exactly that — the old roofing system comes off down to the decking, not just a new layer installed over what's already there. That's the only way to actually inspect the decking, ice and water protection, and underlayment for problems that an overlay would simply seal in. If decking needs to be replaced, that's identified and priced as part of your written estimate, not discovered halfway through the job.

We install asphalt and composite shingles, metal roofing, wood shakes and tile, and we're factory-certified with GAF, IKO, Owens Corning, Malarkey, Atlas, CertainTeed and SRS — which typically means stronger warranty options than an uncertified installer can offer. Every replacement includes a free, no-pressure written estimate up front and a final walkthrough once the work is done.

## Signs It May Be Time To Replace

- Widespread granule loss, with bare or shiny patches across the roof
- Curling, cracking or brittle shingles in many areas, not just one
- Repeated leaks in different spots
- Soft or sagging areas that point to decking problems
- Multiple layers of aging shingles
- Extensive storm damage from [hail](/storm-damage/hail-damage.html) or [wind](/storm-damage/wind-damage.html)
- A roof at or past the age typical for its material. See how long a roof lasts in [Northern Indiana](/learning-center/how-long-does-a-roof-last-in-northern-indiana.html).

If only one area is damaged, [roof repair](/roofing/roof-repair.html) may be enough. Our [repair vs. replacement guide](/learning-center/roof-repair-vs-replacement.html) explains how the decision usually goes.

## How A Replacement Works

1. **Free inspection and written estimate.** We inspect the roof, explain the findings, and put the scope, materials and price in writing.
2. **Choosing materials.** We go through product options, colors and warranty choices for your home.
3. **Scheduling.** We give you a specific timeline before work begins.
4. **Tear-off.** The old roofing comes off down to the decking.
5. **Deck inspection.** We check the decking, and replace anything soft, rotted or damaged. Here's [what happens if bad decking is found](/learning-center/what-happens-if-bad-decking-is-found.html).
6. **Installation.** Underlayment, ice and water protection, flashing, drip edge, shingles or other roofing, and ridge are installed to manufacturer specification.
7. **Cleanup and walkthrough.** We clean the job site daily and do a final walkthrough with you.

Most residential replacements are completed in one to three days, weather permitting. Our guide on [how long a roof replacement takes](/learning-center/how-long-does-roof-replacement-take.html) covers what affects the schedule.

## Choosing Materials

- **Asphalt and composite shingles** are the most common choice in Michiana. See our [asphalt shingle roofing](/roofing/asphalt-shingle-roofing.html) page for 3-tab and architectural options.
- **Metal roofing** is a different look and system, with its own installation details.
- **Wood shakes** offer a natural appearance and need their own maintenance.
- **Tile** is a heavier material that suits certain styles of home.

There's no single right answer for every roof. We'll walk through the tradeoffs for your house, and we'll tell you if a material doesn't suit it.

## What Affects Cost

Replacement cost depends on the size and pitch of the roof, the material you choose, the number of layers being removed, how much decking needs repair, and the complexity of details like valleys, chimneys and skylights. Our guide to [what a new roof costs in Michiana](/learning-center/new-roof-cost-michiana.html) explains these factors, and our guide on [what should be included in a roofing estimate](/learning-center/what-should-be-included-in-a-roofing-estimate.html) shows you what to look for when comparing quotes.

## Timing And Michiana Weather

Roof replacement is scheduled around weather. Rain and snow can delay a job, and cold conditions affect how some materials seal. Some homeowners are surprised to learn a roof can be replaced in winter under the right conditions. See our guide on [replacing a roof in winter in Indiana](/learning-center/can-you-replace-a-roof-in-winter-in-indiana.html) for details.

## Insurance And Storm Damage

If a storm is the reason you're considering replacement, we can document the damage with photos to support a claim you choose to file. The decision on a claim belongs to your insurance carrier, and we can't promise an outcome.

## Preparing For Your Replacement

A little preparation makes the job go smoothly. Move vehicles out of the driveway or away from the house, take fragile items off walls and shelves inside since vibration can loosen them, and let us know about pets, children or anything else we should plan around. We'll go through the plan with you before work starts, and we'll clean up daily.
