Replacing a roof is one of the largest investments a homeowner makes in a property, and it's not a job for shortcuts. No Limit Roofing has handled full residential roof replacements across South Bend, Mishawaka, Plymouth and the greater Michiana region since 2010, and every replacement follows the same process regardless of the size of the home: a full tear-off to the decking, an honest look at what's underneath, and a factory-certified installation from a crew Jonathan Smith personally supervises.

A full replacement means exactly that — the old roofing system comes off down to the decking, not just a new layer installed over what's already there. That's the only way to actually inspect the decking, ice and water protection, and underlayment for problems that an overlay would simply seal in. If decking needs to be replaced, that's identified and priced as part of your written estimate, not discovered halfway through the job.

We install asphalt and composite shingles, metal roofing, wood shakes and tile, and we're factory-certified with GAF, IKO, Owens Corning, Malarkey, Atlas, CertainTeed and SRS — which typically means stronger warranty options than an uncertified installer can offer. Every replacement includes a free, no-pressure written estimate up front and a final walkthrough once the work is done.
